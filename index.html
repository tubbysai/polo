How to Play

  • Hold on the goal to aim; drag to move the aim ring.
  • Release when the power meter is green for the best shot.
  • Corners score bonuses, but posts and misses are riskier.
  • Mix up shots because the goalie learns your favorite zones.
  • Every shot counts: score a goal for AMV, but a block, post, or wide shot hands the point straight to the Visitors.
  • Match is 5 rounds. Ties go to sudden death.
  • Pro difficulty has a 6-second shot clock that starts as soon as you can aim.
